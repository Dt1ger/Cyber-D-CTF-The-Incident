Incident summary: card fraud linked to shop.example
Customers report fraud on cards used only at shop.example since 14 March.
The fraud team sent three theories to the bank and asked the response team to pick one.
Theory one: card numbers were pulled from the order database.
Theory two: card numbers were copied in the shopper's browser as they typed.
Theory three: a store payment terminal was infected.
